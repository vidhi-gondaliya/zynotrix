# ZYNOTRIX — Product Vision

## Vision Statement

**"Make every team's work visible, rewarding, and effortless — powered by AI."**

ZYNOTRIX exists to eliminate the coordination tax that modern teams pay daily: context-switching, status meetings, scattered tools, and the invisible work of keeping everyone aligned. We believe that when teams have one workspace that thinks with them — not just for them — they produce their best work.

---

## Mission

To build the world's most intelligent and human-centered team workspace for growing organizations — one that reduces friction, surfaces risk early, rewards performance, and makes collaboration feel like a superpower instead of overhead.

## North Star Metric

**Weekly Active Projects (WAP)** — the number of projects where at least one task was moved to a new status in the past 7 days.

This metric captures actual workflow usage (not just logins), is resistant to gaming, and correlates strongly with retention and expansion revenue.

---

## Secondary Metrics

- Task completion rate (done / total active tasks)
- Daily active users / Monthly active users ratio (DAU/MAU)
- AI feature engagement rate (% of users who generate at least one AI insight per week)
- Notification read-through rate (captures engagement quality)
- Leaderboard participation rate (rewards system adoption)

## Product Principles

### 1. One Workspace, Not One More Integration
Every feature added to ZYNOTRIX must reduce the need for another tool, not create another integration point. If we add meetings, meeting notes must live in the same space as the tasks that meeting produced.

### 2. AI Must Be Contextual
AI features in ZYNOTRIX always receive workspace context (project data, task counts, team velocity). They never respond in a generic way. The AI knows your project is behind schedule before you tell it.

### 3. Gamification Must Be Earned
Points and badges are awarded for real actions (task completion, early delivery, attendance). There are no "login streak" vanity rewards. The leaderboard reflects contribution, not activity theater.

### 4. Dark Mode Is The Default Experience
The "Obsidian Command" design system is built dark-first. The product feels premium because it was designed for the screen, not the printout.

### 5. Accessibility Is Not Optional
WCAG 2.1 AA compliance is a target for every release. Color contrast, keyboard navigation, and screen reader labels are engineering requirements, not afterthoughts. **[Current status: partial — see `23_Accessibility.md`]**

## Positioning Statement

For digital agency teams and software startups who are drowning in tool sprawl, ZYNOTRIX is the all-in-one AI workspace that consolidates project management, team chat, meetings, documents, attendance, and gamification into a single coherent environment — so teams spend their time building, not coordinating.

Unlike Jira (too complex), Asana (no AI), or Notion (no real project management), ZYNOTRIX is purpose-built for service businesses that ship work to clients and need visibility from brief to delivery.

## What Success Looks Like

A team leader opens ZYNOTRIX in the morning, sees three overdue tasks flagged automatically, asks the AI "what should I focus on today?", gets a prioritized action list, and closes three tasks before lunch — all without opening another app.

A member clocks in, checks their points balance, sees they're 50 points away from a free coffee coupon, and feels motivated to close that task they've been deferring.

A client receives a secure portal link, sees project progress without needing an account, and emails back "looks great, approved" — with zero back-and-forth status calls.

That is the ZYNOTRIX vision realized.
